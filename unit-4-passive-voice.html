Grammar: Active vs Passive

Two Ways to Say the Same Thing

Active (能動態)

The doer is the subject.
Focus on WHO does the action.

Farmers grow rice in Japan.
Passive (受動態)

The object is the subject.
Focus on WHAT happens.

Rice is grown in Japan.

Use passive when you want to focus on the action or the object, not who did it.

Present Passive

is / are + past participle

Present Passive Rule
Subject + is / are + past participle (+ by ...)

"Rice is grown in many parts of Japan."

お米は日本の多くの地域で栽培されています。
Explanation
We use passive because the important information is what happens to the rice, not who grows it. Everybody knows farmers grow rice!

"Sushi is eaten all over the world."

寿司は世界中で食べられています。

"The shinkansen is known for its punctuality."

新幹線は時間正確さで知られています。
Past Passive

was / were + past participle

Past Passive Rule
Subject + was / were + past participle (+ by ...)

"The shinkansen was invented in Japan (1964)."

新幹線は日本で発明されました。

"Mount Fuji was formed by volcanic activity."

富士山は火山活動によって形成されました。

"These temples were built in the 8th century."

これらの寺院は8世紀に建てられました。

Speaking Practice

How Things Are Done in Japan

1. How is matcha tea prepared in a traditional ceremony?

What tools are used? Is the process slow or fast?
Model Answer
First, hot water is poured into a bowl. Then, matcha powder is added with a bamboo scoop. The tea is whisked with a bamboo whisk until it becomes frothy. Every movement is done slowly and carefully.

2. How are festivals celebrated in your city?

What food is served? What music is played?
Model Answer
In my city, the summer festival is celebrated with fireworks. Yatai stalls are set up along the river, and traditional food like takoyaki is served. At night, a huge bonfire is lit and everyone dances around it.

3. What is served at a traditional Japanese breakfast?

How is it different from a Western breakfast?
Model Answer
A traditional Japanese breakfast is served with rice, miso soup, grilled fish, and pickled vegetables. Nori (seaweed) is often served on the side, and green tea is always served hot.

Amazing Facts About Japan

These things are all connected to Japan! Can you use passive voice to talk about them?

Matcha
Matcha is used in traditional tea ceremonies. It is made from finely ground green tea leaves.
Shinkansen
The bullet train was invented in Japan. It is known for being the fastest train in the world.
Sumo
Sumo wrestling has been practised for centuries. Tournaments are held six times a year.
Origami
Origami is made by folding paper into shapes. Cranes are folded as symbols of peace.
Karaoke
Karaoke was invented in Japan. Songs are sung in small rooms with friends.
Ramen
Ramen is eaten all over Japan. It is made with noodles, broth, and various toppings.
