Warm-Up

What Sticks

Revision is not rereading everything. It is finding what you nearly forgot.

Which word from this year do you still use without thinking?

Go further: Where did you first meet it?

Possible answer
Memorable. I first used it in the mid-year review and it stayed.

What grammar point do you check twice before you write?

Go further: Why do you distrust that one?

Possible answer
Reported speech. The pronouns move around and I still get lost.
Quick Review

Four Patterns, One Answer

Pick the pattern that fits. Good revision means choosing, not collecting.

Use present perfect continuous to describe a habit from this year.

Go further: What does the tense show that a simple past sentence would not?

Possible answer
I have been keeping a word notebook since April. Simple past would make it sound finished.

Report one sentence a friend said this week.

Go further: What did you change besides the tense?

Possible answer
He said he was tired. I changed I to he and left out his joke.
Picture Fluency

Describe, Then Extend

Describe the picture in twenty seconds. Then answer a question that takes it somewhere else.

A photo shows a crowded crossing in the rain. Describe it in three sentences.

Go further: What might happen next for one person in that crowd?

Possible answer
Many umbrellas fill the crossing and the lights reflect on the road. One person steps forward, then stops. A student behind him laughs and moves around.

A photo shows an empty classroom after the final bell. What details tell the story?

Go further: Who was the last person to leave?

Possible answer
A chair out of place and a jacket on the back of a desk. The last person was in a hurry.

A photo shows a market stall with one unusual object. Describe it without naming it.

Go further: Why would the owner keep such a thing?

Possible answer
It is small and wooden with a strange shape, and it sits beside the fruit. The owner keeps it because customers ask about it.

Planning Practice

Outline a Talk

Four slides, four sentences each. Talk for two minutes without reading.

What topic would you happily talk about for two minutes?

Go further: What would you say in the first twenty seconds?

Possible answer
My favourite place in town. I would start with the moment you turn the corner and see it.

Which slide would be hardest to fill?

Go further: What detail could save it?

Possible answer
The middle slide. One specific sound from the place would give me enough to say.
Roleplay

The Interview

Your partner plays a visitor to your town. Answer their questions with detail.

Where would you take a visitor for one hour?

Go further: What would you deliberately not show them?

Possible answer
The old bookshop near the station. I would not show them the mall because it looks like every other mall.

What question do you hope they ask?

Go further: How would you start your answer?

Possible answer
I hope they ask what I do after school. I would start with the walk home.

Challenge

Two Minutes, No Notes

Talk about your topic. If you stop, take a breath and continue. Do not restart.

What will you do if you forget your next point?

Go further: Which phrase buys you time without sounding like filler?

Possible answer
I will say let me think for a second. It sounds better than um.

How will you know your talk is long enough?

Go further: What will you cut if it runs short?

Possible answer
I will check the clock after each slide. I will cut the second example, not the story.
Go Deeper

Ready Enough

Preparation is a tool, not a shield.

Do you prepare more when you feel nervous or when you feel confident?

Go further: Which kind of preparation actually helps?

Possible answer
When I am nervous I over-prepare and forget it all. Knowing my first sentence helps most.

What is the difference between memorising a talk and knowing it?

Go further: Which one do you do?

Possible answer
Memorising is word for word. Knowing is being able to start again from any point. I try to know it.

Who in your class speaks well without seeming to prepare?

Go further: What do they do that you could copy?

Possible answer
My friend Aoi. She pauses and looks at people instead of rushing, so she seems calm.
Wrap-Up

Your Two-Minute Plan

Topic, four slides, one question to ask the class at the end.

What question will you ask your classmates?

Go further: Why that question?

Possible answer
I will ask where they would go in town if they had one free hour. It starts a real conversation.
