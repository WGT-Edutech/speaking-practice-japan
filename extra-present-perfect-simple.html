Warm-Up

Have You Ever...?

Answer these questions with a partner. Use "Yes, I have" or "No, I haven't."

1. Have you ever eaten something strange at a festival?

Model Answer
Yes, I have! I have tried takoyaki and chocolate-covered bananas at school festivals. They were delicious!

2. Have you ever watched a movie without subtitles in English?

Model Answer
No, I haven't. But I have watched anime with English subtitles, and I have understood most of it!

3. Have you ever played a sport in another country?

Model Answer
Yes, I have. I have played soccer at a park in Australia with my cousin's friends.

4. Have you ever sung a song at karaoke in front of strangers?

Model Answer
Yes, I have! I have sung "Lemon" by Kenshi Yonezu in front of my class. Everyone clapped!

5. Have you ever travelled on the Shinkansen for more than 3 hours?

Model Answer
Yes, I have. I have ridden the Shinkansen from Tokyo to Osaka for about 2.5 hours. The view from the window was amazing!
Grammar: Present Perfect Simple

Actions With a Connection to Now

Present Perfect Simple Rule
Subject + have/has + past participle

Past
I visited Tokyo. → I have visited Tokyo.
Past
She finished her homework. → She has finished her homework.
Past
They saw the new anime. → They have seen the new anime.
Now
We have learned English for 3 years!

Use this tense to talk about experiences, completed actions, and things that started in the past and continue now.

What Have You Seen?

Look at both pictures. Use present perfect to compare what you can see.

Picture 1: Football
Picture 2: Guitar

1. In Picture 1, what is the man doing on the field? Have you ever celebrated a win like that?

2. In Picture 2, what is the person doing? Have you ever tried to play a string instrument?

3. Which of these two activities is usually done as a team? Have you ever played in a team?

4. The man in Picture 1 is out on his own. Have you ever played a sport without a team?

5. If you could try one of these, which would you choose? Have you ever done the other one?

Model Answer
In Picture 1, I can see one man on a football field. He is wearing a red shirt with the number 10 on it, and he has just raised his fist, so his team has probably just scored. In Picture 2, I can see one man playing a guitar on a street. I have played football in a team for three years, but I have never tried to play the guitar. If I could try one of these, I would choose the guitar, because I have wanted to learn for a long time.
