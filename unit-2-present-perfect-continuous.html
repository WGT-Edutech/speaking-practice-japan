Grammar: Present Perfect Continuous

Actions That Continue Until Now

Present Perfect Continuous Rule
Subject + have/has + been + verb-ing

June 2020
Started studying English
July 2022
Started playing baseball
January 2024
Started watching anime
NOW
Still doing all three!

Use this tense to talk about actions that started in the past and are still happening.

For vs Since

How Long Have You Been...?

Time Expressions
For + a period of time (3 years, 2 months, a week)
Since + a point in time (2020, June, last summer)

"I have been studying English for 4 years."

私は4年間英語を勉強しています。

"She has been playing the piano since 2019."

彼女は2019年からピアノを弾いています。

"We have been waiting for 30 minutes!"

私たちは30分も待っています！
